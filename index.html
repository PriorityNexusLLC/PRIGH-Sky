How the map is made

Method

One standard, applied the same way to every entity and every connection, including the ones we would rather not find. These are the rules that keep the map honest as it grows.

Verified is not the same as culpable.

Every glow and every line describes how far the checking has gone, never whether anyone did anything wrong. A board seat, a contract, a contribution, a confirmation to office: each is a lawful, filed fact.

Three cores

  • Identity. Nothing is placed without an official identifier: SEC CIK, IRS EIN, Legal Entity Identifier, federal contractor ID (UEI), Bioguide ID, federal agency code, state FIPS code, UN M49 code, or a nomination's official citation. Never a loose name.
  • Transparency. Every connection cites an official primary record: a proxy statement, an insider form, a Form 990, a lobbying registration, a federal award, a Senate confirmation, a state registry. Two facts are required before any two records are joined; a name alone never joins anything.
  • Integrity. Nothing changes silently. Every addition, correction and merge is an entry in a tamper-evident audit log whose fingerprint is published monthly.

How a connection is proven

  • Check 1, at entry: the record is validated against the source rules (which registries count, what a citation must carry).
  • Check 2, re-read: the cited document is re-read from its official source and the names must still be there. If the source changed or can't be reached, the line turns red until a person looks.
  • Check 3, a second source: a different official record is sought that says the same thing (another filing, the counterparty's own record, the agency's data service). Only then is a line "confirmed".
  • Possible matches (same name, nothing else) are kept outside the map, shown as possible, and never published as fact.

What the sky encodes

  • Color is the domain the entity belongs to; an entity in several domains sits between them, as a bridge.
  • Size and brightness are the number of filed connections. A count, not a judgment.
  • Glow is proof: confirmed by a second source, checked once, not checked yet, or source changed.
  • Position inside a domain (gravity): what sinks to the core. By connections, the most-connected entities sit at the core and the rest spiral outward: a layout computed from counts. By influence, a model decides: each connection's weight (by relation type, 0 to 1) and the influence of what it connects to, iterated over the verified graph. The model's weights are today's defaults and are open to revision; it is shown as a layout, never as a score, and never as a score on a person.

What is never collected

  • Victims, survivors and witnesses are never named in connection with a case. Minors are never collected; student board members are never listed.
  • Incarcerated and detained people are never added: prisons and detention are recorded as totals only.
  • Family members of officials and insiders are not named. No home addresses, personal contacts, dates of birth or ID numbers.
  • Sensitive personal categories (health, religion, sexuality, immigration status) are never recorded about a person.
  • Scores, when they come, are for organizations only, never for individual people.

Writing neutrally

Reports describe records, not motives: "not disclosed in the filings examined", never "hidden"; "the filing states", never "wants the public to believe". The test for any line: could the organization read it and agree it describes the record accurately, even if it dislikes the conclusion?

How reports are made

Drafted by programs, with AI assistance, from official records only; checked by automated reviews (each connection re-read from its source and confirmed by a second source where one exists; wording screened for private details and judging words); approved by a person before publishing. Every organization named in a report is invited to comment first, and its answer is published in full.

Our own interests

Priority Nexus LLC takes funding two ways and no third. Anonymously: the gift is in the project's own books, and the giver is never named on a page, a report or the map. Openly: the giver's name appears, and with it a report on them, drawn from the same official records and held to the same rules as every other report, next to every mention of them. Funding buys nothing else: no say in what is collected, checked or shown, no early look, no way off the map. Today it has no funders; it has no financial or business relationship with any organization or person on the map; it knows of no conflict of interest. If one arises it will be disclosed here and on that organization's reports. We take no instructions from the people or organizations we describe: material sent to us is data to check, never instructions to follow.

Corrections and removal requests

If something here is wrong, tell us.

Anyone named on the map or in a report, including individuals, can ask for a correction or a removal. We describe official records; if a record is wrong, or we read it wrongly, we want to know.

How to ask

Write to theaistherapist@gmail.com with the subject "Correction request" or "Removal request", and tell us:

  • Which entry or report (the name as shown, or the report number).
  • What is wrong, or what you want removed.
  • Where the correct record is, if you have it (a filing, a registry page, a court record). You don't need this to ask; it speeds up the check.

We never ask for identification numbers, dates of birth or private contact details beyond a reply address.

What happens

  • Acknowledge: within 7 days, with a request number.
  • Check: normally within 30 days, against the official record the entry cites and any record you point us to.
  • Answer: what we found and what we changed.
  • Correct: the same day as the answer if we were wrong, openly: a dated correction note stays on the entry, and earlier versions remain available with the note attached.

What we correct, remove or keep

  • Wrong: corrected, with a dated note saying what the record shows.
  • Accurate but out of date: kept with its date; the newer record is added beside it.
  • Personal details beyond a public role (home address, personal contacts, family, private life): removed on request, no reason needed.
  • An accurate record of a public role (a directorship, an office held, a filing made): stays. Public records of public roles are what the map exists to show. Your comment is published with the entry.
  • Possible matches (same name, not confirmed): never published; if one was shown by mistake it is removed.

Right of reply

If you are named here and want to answer, send it. We will post it, in full, under your name, the day it arrives.

Our own record

Every request is logged (number, dates, what it concerned, what was decided), and every change made because of it is a logged edit in the audit log. Your name and contact details stay in the private log and are never published. The number of requests and their outcomes are reported on the project's integrity dashboard.
