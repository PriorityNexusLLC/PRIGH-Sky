Three rules

  • Identity. A thing is what its own register says it is. No record, no entry. A name alone never joins two records.
  • Transparency. Every tie carries the filing it came from. Open the same record and see the same thing.
  • Integrity. Nothing changes quietly. Every change is logged and the log is fingerprinted daily.
